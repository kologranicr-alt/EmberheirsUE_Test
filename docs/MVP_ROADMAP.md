# EMBERHEIRS — MVP ROADMAP

This roadmap is directional. `CURRENT_PHASE.md` is authoritative for what may be implemented now.

## UE Evaluation — Proof of Concept
City-builder camera; Landscape; PCG forest; river/water; Central Camp; one citizen; one tree resource; physical Wood gather/carry/deliver loop; performance/workflow evaluation.

## Foundation after migration approval
Simulation clock; stable IDs; definition registry/data model; versioned save/load; logging/debug tools; automated test harness.

## World & population
Procedural starting region v1; Central Camp; 10 survivors; selection/info; Food/Wood/Stone deposits and inventories.

## Physical work & construction
Manual temporary work orders; gathering/cargo/delivery; construction placement; materials; builder labor; completion.

## Workforce
Profession/workplace foundation; automatic employment only when completed workplaces exist; workplace-generated tasks; action execution.

## Citizen life
Food/hunger/eating; housing/sleep/rest; firewood/basic warmth; deterministic priorities/autonomy.

## Failure/recovery
Understandable shortages, consequences, recovery paths, and player-facing explanations.

## Functional Village Gate
30–60 minute playable settlement without debug tools. Player can build and sustain a village, or fail for legible reasons. Save/load reliable.

## Later, not MVP
Seasons/winter/weather; illness/medicine depth; profession XP/levels and secondary proficiency; multi-region world; outposts; local logistics networks; carts/trucks/rail; zoning; money/wages/taxes; private economy; factions; generations; institutions; advanced eras.
